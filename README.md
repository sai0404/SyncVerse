
# SyncVerse
**previously called as LRC_to_FCPXML**

Make your Lyrical video songs from given lrc ( lyrics with time-synced) singing content perfectly.
I'm presently made only for final cut pro where you can download your time-synced generator(normal one) by giving input as video's .lrc or .srt version.


**Try it here :** https://sai0404.github.io/SyncVerse/



**User guide:**

**step 1:** As per the video, prepare the sound's .lrc or .srt version.(contins time stamps with scripts).

**step 2:** Upload that file(lrc/srt) and click the button(convert to fcpxml).

**Eg:** Lrc:

			[00:00:00] start
            [00:04:00] vocals
                  .      .
                  .      .
                  .      .
            [00:07:00] end

	
**step 3:** Download the file or copy and save the content using .fcpxml extension to the file.

**step 4:** Final_cutPro->file->import->XML->select this .fcpxml file.

**step 5:** *== Success ==* 

Latest version is integrated with WisperX through API, for better experience make **offset=-1500ms** output will be in word - word in .lrc version.
